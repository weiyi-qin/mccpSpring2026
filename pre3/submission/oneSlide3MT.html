3MT | Double-Q research story

What if your GPS had to drive before today's road rules were posted?

My research builds a two-signal decision system that helps online services keep moving without "running red lights" when rules keep changing.

The challenge

  • Real systems make decisions step by step.
  • But today's rules may only appear after the choice is made.
  • And some mistakes cannot be undone later.

The idea behind Double-Q

Instead of using one warning light, I use two. One watches progress. The other watches risk. Together, they aim to keep decisions efficient and safe across both calm and fast-changing conditions.

Presenter: Weiyi Qin
Topic: safer online decisions under changing rules
If we can make better decisions under changing rules, we can build more reliable scheduling, resource-allocation, and fraud-detection systems.
